# Requirements of privacy

The rows that the skill `r-privacy` owns. `collection.md` says what the status means.

| Id | Title | Status | Statement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <a id="r-32"></a>R-32 | Privacy questions | in force | `r-privacy` applies when the change stores, logs, exports, or shows personal data. `r-plan` asks three questions. What personal data is stored, shown, logged, or exported? What remains if a log or a fixture is copied out of the machine? Who asked for the export? `r-review` checks three things. Logs, errors, and fixtures contain no personal data. An export is one the user asked for in this slice. Example data is marked as an example. Real data is not in the tree. This applies to both, when the skill is installed. `r-privacy` owns the questions. |
